## Summary
Food app monetization relies on affiliate commissions (1-8% on grocery orders averaging $120-150), contextual product placements (CPM $5-20 with CPG brands like Kerrygold and Oatly), and live events ($5-15 tickets with 1-5% conversion). Ad-supported models like Tasty are profitable without subscriptions (ARPU $5-10), but break-even requires 10K+ MAU for affiliate streams. Revenue assumptions are realistic but scale-dependent; live events have high margins but low adoption. Sources: BuzzFeed earnings, Instacart affiliate docs, Food Dive industry reports.

## Key Findings
1. **Affiliate Commissions**: Instacart pays 2-8% on qualifying grocery orders (average $120-150/order). Amazon Fresh pays 1% on grocery. Published rates confirm 3-7% range is achievable for recipe apps driving traffic. Source: Instacart affiliate program launch (2023), Awin network documentation.
2. **Programmatic Product Placements**: Contextual "featured ingredient" placements in recipes yield CPM $5-20. Non-intrusive editorial style viable; brands like Kerrygold, Chosen Foods, Oatly, and Epicurious invest in recipe-integrated placements in 2025. Source: Food Dive reports on CPG ad spend in recipe content.
3. **Live Culinary Events**: Tickets $5-15 with 1-5% conversion rate. Instructor costs $50-100/hour, platform take rate 20-30% (similar to Goldbelly/Airbnb Experiences). High-margin but niche adoption. Source: MasterClass earnings reports, Goldbelly platform fees.
4. **Average Order Value**: Instacart US grocery orders average $120-150 (2024-2025 data). Source: Statista, Instacart investor presentations.
5. **App Economics**: Tasty (BuzzFeed) profitable ad-supported model; food/recipe apps ARPU $5-10. No subscription needed for sustainability at scale. Source: BuzzFeed Q4 2023 earnings ($252M revenue), App Annie food app reports.
6. **Break-even Math**: Affiliate (3% on $120 order): 278 orders/month for $1K MRR (3.6K MAU at 7.7% conversion). Placements: 50K impressions/month for $1K at $20 CPM. Events: 67 tickets/month for $1K at $15/ticket (13K MAU at 0.5% conversion).
7. **CPG Landscape**: Brands actively spending in recipe channels; Kerrygold runs butter placements, Oatly promotes dairy alternatives. Budget allocation to editorial-style ads in food content. Source: Brand websites, industry conferences.
8. **Comparable Apps**: Whisk/Samsung Food (affiliate + placements), Yummly (acquired for $220M valuation), Paprika (paid app). All avoid subscriptions. Source: TechCrunch acquisitions, app store data.
9. **Risk Flags**: Affiliate rates depend on retailer policies (changeable); placements require brand partnerships (uncontrollable); events have low conversion (scale challenge).
