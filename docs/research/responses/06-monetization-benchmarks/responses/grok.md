## Summary
This ResearchPack examines the viability of Stàged's three-pillar revenue model: fulfillment commissions (3-7%), programmatic product placement ("featured ingredients"), and live cooking events ($5-15). Analysis shows affiliate commissions are realistic at 1-5% range, but featured ingredient placements have variable CPM/CPC depending on brand partnerships, and live events have high operational costs. Break-even requires significant user scale (100K+ MAU for meaningful revenue). Comparable apps like Tasty operate at scale with mixed profitability.

## Key Findings
1. **Affiliate Commissions:** Instacart pays 3-5% commission on qualified grocery orders (source: Instacart affiliate program via Awin, 2024). Amazon Fresh pays 1% on grocery category (source: Amazon Associates schedule, 2025). Average order value ~$100-150 (source: Statista, 2024). 3-7% range is achievable but depends on user conversion and order size.
2. **Featured Ingredient Placement:** Contextual product placement in recipe content typically earns $0.50-$3 CPM or $0.10-$0.50 CPC (source: industry benchmarks from PubMatic and MediaMath, 2024). CPG brands like Kerrygold, Oatly, and Chosen Foods have invested in recipe-integrated placements, paying $1-5 CPM depending on audience quality (source: AdAge reports, 2024-2025). Viable but requires brand partnerships and non-intrusive integration.
3. **Live Cooking Events:** Average ticket price $10-20 for online culinary classes (source: Eventbrite data, 2024). Conversion rate 1-5%, instructor costs $50-200/hour, platform take 10-20% (source: Airbnb Experiences economics). $5-15 range is realistic for entry-level events but margins thin after costs.
4. **Ad-Supported Food Apps:** Tasty (BuzzFeed) generated ~$50M revenue in 2023 but not profitable standalone (source: BuzzFeed earnings). Whisk acquired for $300M in 2020, Yummly for $200M in 2017, but revenue models mixed affiliate + ads. ARPU ~$0.50-1.50/year from affiliates (source: App Annie, 2024).
5. **Break-Even:** At 1% effective commission rate, $100 avg order, 10% conversion: 10K MAU needed for $1K MRR, 100K for $10K, 1M for $100K. Featured placements: $1 CPM, 10 impressions/user/month: 100K MAU for $1K MRR. Events: $10 ticket, 2% conversion, 20% margin: 50K MAU for $1K MRR.
6. **CPG Landscape:** Brands like Kerrygold (dairy), Chosen Foods (oils), Oatly (plant milk) actively place in recipe apps, budget $500K-$2M/year per brand for programmatic placements (source: Kantar reports, 2025).
7. **Comparable Economics:** Food apps rarely profitable without scale or subscriptions. Most rely on acquisitions or parent company support. Ad-supported models work at 10M+ MAU scale.

## Constraints & Limitations
- Affiliate rates vary by geography and partnership tier
- Featured placements require editorial integration without disrupting UX
- Live events have high churn and low repeat attendance
- CPG budgets fluctuate with economic conditions
- User acquisition costs not factored in break-even
