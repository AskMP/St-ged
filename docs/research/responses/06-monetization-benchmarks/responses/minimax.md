## 1. Revenue Model Reality Check

### 1.1 Fulfillment Affiliate Commissions (Instacart/Amazon Fresh)

**Claim:** 3-7% affiliate fee from Instacart/Amazon Fresh per order

**Reality:** ⚠️ **SIGNIFICANTLY OVERSTATED**

| Platform | Actual Commission Rate | Source |
|----------|----------------------|--------|
| **Instacart** | 3% revshare OR $2-10 CPA (per activation) | UpPromote, Impact, Instacart official docs |
| **Amazon Fresh** | 1% | Amazon Associates Commission Schedule (Nov 2025) |
| **Amazon Grocery** | 1% | Amazon Associates Commission Schedule |

**Key Findings:**
- Instacart's official program via Impact offers: "up to $10 CPA or cost-per-acquisition commission for every new customer order" OR "up to 15% on qualifying purchases" (though this appears to be upper tier/private)
- Most publicly available rates: **3% revenue share** or **$2-10 per lead**
- Amazon Fresh/Grocery: **1% commission** (as of November 2025)
- Note: Amazon previously slashed commissions by ~66% in April 2020 — further cuts are possible

**Dollar Value Calculation (using realistic rates):**
- Average Instacart order: ~$80-100 (industry estimates for 2024-2025)
- At 3%: $2.40-3.00 per order
- At $5 CPA (average): $5.00 per new customer (one-time)

**Risk Factor:** HIGH — The 3-7% claim is not supported by public Instacart documentation. Realistic expectation: 1-3% or $2-10 CPA.

---

### 1.2 Programmatic Product Placement ("Featured Ingredients")

**Claim:** "Featured Ingredients" integrated into recipes — viable, non-intrusive ad format with meaningful CPM

**Reality:** ✅ **VIABLE BUT REQUIRES SCALE**

**CPM Benchmarks (2024-2025):**

| Ad Format | Typical CPM | Notes |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Display ads (standard) | $1-3 | Requires 50K+ monthly visitors |
| In-recipe contextual (Chicory network) | $5-15 | Premium contextual placement |
| Premium sponsored recipe | $50-200 | Direct brand deals |
| Recipe card takeover | $100-500 | High-engagement placement |

**Market Context:**
- Chicory's network: 5,200+ recipe sites
- 85% of shoppers refer to online recipes while grocery shopping (P2PI Contextual Commerce Shopper Study)
- CPG brands actively investing: Kerrygold, Oatly, Kraft, Chosen Foods
- Average ticket price for Airbnb Experiences (cooking classes): **$66** — indicates premium event pricing

**CPM Reality Check:**
- A recipe page with 10,000 monthly views at $10 CPM = $100/month
- At $50 CPM (premium placement) = $500/month
- Need 100K+ monthly views for meaningful revenue ($1,000+/month)

**Risk Factor:** MEDIUM — Viable model but requires significant traffic. "Meaningful CPM" requires negotiation or network participation (Chicory, etc.).

---

### 1.3 Stàge Events (Live Cooking Sessions)

**Claim:** $5-15 entry fee, viable high-margin revenue stream

**Reality:** ⚠️ **MARGIN RISK — FEASIBLE BUT TIGHT**

**Comparable Benchmarks:**

| Platform | Take Rate / Host Payout | Ticket Range |
|----------|------------------------|--------------|
| Airbnb Experiences | ~20-30% platform fee (host keeps 70-80%) | Avg $66 for cooking classes |
| ClassPass | ~25-30% | $15-50 typical |
| Eventbrite | ~10-12% + $1.50/ticket | Wide range |

**Event Economics Calculation:**

| Scenario | Ticket Price | Platform Fee (25%) | Host Payout | Net to Stàged |
|----------|--------------|-------------------|-------------|---------------|
| Low | $5 | $1.25 | $3.75 | $1.25 |
| Mid | $10 | $2.50 | $7.50 | $2.50 |
| High | $15 | $3.75 | $11.25 | $3.75 |

**Break-Even Analysis:**
- At $10/ticket with 25% take rate: $2.50/event attendee
- To earn $1,000/month: Need 400 paid attendees/month
- To earn $10,000/month: Need 4,000 paid attendees/month

**Risk Factor:** MEDIUM-HIGH — Low ticket prices ($5-15) combined with platform fees leave thin margins. Requires high volume or upselling.

### 1.4 Free, Ad-Supported Model Sustainability

**Claim:** Free to users, all revenue from partnerships/events, no subscription

**Reality:** ✅ **FEASIBLE BUT CHALLENGING**

**Market Evidence:**
- Recipe apps: 73.6% are free (Market.us 2025)
- Recipe app market: $724.4M (2024) → $2.27B (2033), 13.5% CAGR
- Food blogger median income: ~$9,169/month (RankIQ survey of 803 bloggers)
- Display ads = 42-44% of food blog revenue; CPM $1-3 at 50K monthly visitors

**ARPU Benchmarks:**

| Monetization Type | Typical ARPU | Notes |
|-------------------|--------------|-------|
| Display ads only | $0.50-2.00/MAU/month | Requires 50K+ MAU for ad networks |
| Affiliate (grocery) | $0.10-0.50/MAU/month | Highly variable |
| Mixed (ads + affiliate) | $1.00-3.00/MAU/month | At scale |
| Premium events | $0.50-2.00/MAU/month | If 5-10% conversion to paid events |

**Risk Factor:** LOW-MEDIUM — Model is proven in market, but requires substantial MAU (500K-1M+) for sustainability.

## 2. Break-Even Math

### Estimated MAU Needed by Revenue Stream

**Assumptions:**
- Conservative conversion: 2-5% of MAU complete a grocery transaction via affiliate per month
- Average order value: $80 (Instacart)
- Event conversion: 3-5% of MAU purchase an event ticket annually
- Ad RPM: $5 (blended for display + contextual)

### Revenue Stream Breakdown:

| Revenue Target | Affiliate (3% on $80 AOV) | Events ($10 avg, 25% take) | Ads ($5 RPM) | Total MAU Needed |
|---------------|---------------------------|---------------------------|--------------|-----------------|
| $1,000/month | 42,000 monthly orders | 400 attendees/month | 200K page views | ~50K MAU |
| $10,000/month | 420,000 monthly orders | 4,000 attendees/month | 2M page views | ~500K MAU |
| $100,000/month | 4.2M monthly orders | 40,000 attendees/month | 20M page views | ~5M MAU |

### Realistic Scenario (Combined Streams):

| MRR Target | Conservative MAU | Optimistic MAU | Notes |
|------------|------------------|----------------|-------|
| $1K/mo | 75,000 | 30,000 | Blend of all 3 streams |
| $10K/mo | 750,000 | 300,000 | Requires significant scale |
| $100K/mo | 7.5M | 3M | Serious traction required |

**Risk Factor:** HIGH — Break-even requires 30K-75K MAU for $1K/month, which is non-trivial for a new app.

## 5. Risk Flags

### Most Fragile Assumptions

| Rank | Assumption | Risk Level | Reasoning |
|------|------------|------------|-----------|
| 1 | 3-7% Instacart commission | **CRITICAL** | Actual rate is 3% or $2-10 CPA — not 7% |
| 2 | MAU-to-revenue conversion | **HIGH** | 2-5% grocery conversion is optimistic |
| 3 | Event margin at $5-15 | **HIGH** | Platform fees + hosting costs = thin margin |
| 4 | CPM for featured ingredients | **MEDIUM** | Premium CPM ($50+) requires negotiation/scale |
| 5 | CPG brand budget availability | **MEDIUM** | Competitive landscape, requires sales |

### Uncontrollable Factors

- **Amazon commission cuts:** History of dramatic cuts (2020: -66%)
- **Instacart partner program changes:** Rates can fluctuate
- **Ad market volatility:** CPMs fluctuate with economy
- **Platform dependency:** If Instacart/Amazon change terms
- **User acquisition costs:** Rising CAC in competitive food app space

---

## 6. Recommendations

### Immediate Revisions Needed

1. **Revise commission assumptions:**
   - Instacart: Use 3% or $2-10 CPA, not 3-7%
   - Amazon Fresh: Use 1%, not 7%

2. **Recalculate break-even:**
   - $1K/month requires ~50K MAU (not 10K)
   - $10K/month requires ~500K MAU

3. **Event pricing review:**
   - Consider $15-25 minimum to maintain margins after platform fees
   - Or build own ticketing to avoid 25% platform cut

### Strategic Opportunities

1. **Direct CPG partnerships:** Bypass networks, negotiate 10-20% of media spend directly
2. **Retail media integration:** Kroger Precision Marketing, Walmart Connect
3. **First-party data:** Build grocery list data asset for targeting premium

### Conservative Financial Model

| Metric | Conservative | Optimistic |
|--------|-------------|------------|
| Instacart conversion | 1% of MAU | 3% of MAU |
| Commission rate | 3% ($2.40/order) | 5% ($4.00/order) |
| AOV | $80 | $100 |
| Event conversion (annual) | 2% of MAU | 5% of MAU |
| Event take rate | 20% | 30% |
| Ad RPM | $3 | $8 |

**Bottom line:** The model is viable but needs to be stress-tested with realistic rates. The 3-7% commission assumption is the biggest red flag and should be revised immediately.
