## 4. SURPRISING FINDINGS

### Finding 1: Meal Kits Can Be Cheaper Than Groceries
CNET's analysis found that **EveryPlate ($6/serving) is essentially equal to grocery cost ($5.40)** — and in some cases, meal kits with high-end proteins (steak, salmon) are **cheaper** than buying the same ingredients at retail.

**Implication for Stàged:** The "meal kits are expensive" positioning may be outdated. The value proposition has shifted.

---

### Finding 2: Home Cooking Is Actually INCREASING
Despite assumptions of "reversion to delivery," data shows:
- Campbell's (June 2025): "Consumers are cooking at home at the **highest levels since early 2020**"
- NFRA survey (Dec 2023): **81% of Americans** cook more than half their meals at home

**Implication for Stàged:** The home-cooking trend is not reversing — it's **strengthening**. This is a tailwind, not a headwind.

---

### Finding 3: Recipe App Market Is Substantial But Free-Dominated
- Global recipe app market: **$724M-$1.8B (2024)** → projected $2.2B-$4.2B by 2033-2034
- 73%+ of users are on **free** apps
- Paid recipe apps (Paprika, etc.) represent a **tiny niche**

**Implication for Stàged:** The "recipe app" space is growing but monetizing is difficult. Stàged's grocery integration + affiliate model may be more viable than pure recipe apps.

---

### Finding 4: HelloFresh Is Actively Trying to Solve Retention
- Launched €300M "ReFresh" strategy in late 2024
- Expanded menu to 100+ options
- Investing heavily in AI personalization and ready-to-eat meals
- Shift from "acquire-and-churn" to retention-focused

**Implication for Stàged:** The incumbent is vulnerable. Their weakness (churn) is Stàged's opportunity (lower commitment, household coordination).

---

### Finding 5: Eco-Conscious Consumers Are Predominantly Young
- 68% of consumers made more sustainable food choices in past year (L.E.K.)
- Millennials + Gen Z **lead** sustainable purchasing (Clear Channel UK)
- 90% of Millennials/Gen Z prioritize healthy food spending even in crisis (Capital One)

**Implication for Stàged:** Zero-waste positioning resonates most with your target demo (young households).
