## Staged Market Validation Report (As of 2026-03-06)

### Bottom line
`Staged` has a credible problem thesis, but several headline claims need correction. The strongest support is for category turbulence (Blue Apron), continued meal-kit scale (TAM), and packaging complexity. The weakest support is for exact churn percentages, recipe-app retention stats, and the specific "14.5M+ tons meal-kit packaging waste" framing.

### Key Evidence Extracts

1. **Blue Apron deterioration before acquisition**
- Q3'23 10-Q disclosed declining operating metrics versus earlier periods (orders/customers down from prior levels), and lower revenue versus prior-year period. [1]
- 2023-11-13 8-K confirms tender/merger completion at `$13/share`, company became wholly owned by Wonder, and Nasdaq delisting steps initiated. [2]

2. **U.S. consumer food spend normalization after pandemic**
- USDA ERS FES provides annual national FAH/FAFH data through 2024. [4][5]
- Computed FAFH share trend from the downloaded USDA series (all purchasers, with taxes/tips):
  `2019: 56.3%` -> `2020: 51.2%` -> `2021: 55.0%` -> `2022: 56.5%` -> `2023: 58.2%` -> `2024: 58.9%`.
  Interpretation: restaurant/out-of-home share rebounded and exceeded pre-pandemic levels.

3. **Packaging context**
- EPA: containers/packaging had the highest plastic tonnage, "over 14.5 million tons" (2018) across the U.S. MSW framework. [3]
- This is useful context, but **not** a meal-kit-only number.

4. **Price positioning signals**
- Blue Apron menu examples show many meals around ~`$11.69-$15.99/serv` (sample cards on homepage/menu feed). [7]
- Home Chef states "starting at `$7.99` per serving" and FAQ says standard meals average around `$9.99/serv` (varies by menu/order size). [8]
- EveryPlate heavily promotes discounted first-box pricing (`$2.99/meal` offer framing), underscoring promo-driven acquisition economics rather than steady-state pricing. [11]

5. **TAM triangulation**
- Grand View reports global meal-kit market at `$32.8B` in 2024, North America share `45.54%`, and U.S. share `86%` of North America. [6]
- Implied U.S. 2024 size ~= `32.8 * 0.4554 * 0.86 = ~$12.9B`.

---

### Contradictions / Surprises

- **Waste tradeoff is two-sided**: Home Chef cites a Journal of Cleaner Production reference claiming households waste less food when cooking from meal kits, while packaging overhead remains visible. This suggests "waste" is not one-dimensional (packaging vs food spoilage). [10]
- **Category maturity vs public-market outcomes**: Blue Apron's standalone public trajectory was weak, but broader market estimates still show sizable and growing demand, especially across formats (cook-and-eat + heat-and-eat). [1][2][6]

---

### What Is Unverifiable From Current Evidence

- A defensible **industry-wide monthly churn** figure (e.g., 15-20%) with transparent methodology.
- A rigorous **recipe/meal-planning app TAM + retention** benchmark set suitable for investment-grade claims.
- A meal-kit-specific national tonnage estimate directly tied to "14.5M+ tons."

---

### Recommendation for `Staged` Positioning

Use this tighter, evidence-safe narrative:
- "Meal kits face a real packaging-friction perception problem and price-value tension in a promo-heavy market."
- "Category incumbents show mixed outcomes: large TAM potential but execution/churn risks are material."
- "`Staged` can compete by reducing perceived packaging burden and delivering better value transparency and retention design."

Avoid hard numeric claims unless you can secure:
1. third-party churn benchmarks,
2. meal-kit-specific packaging tonnage estimates,
3. app retention panel data.
