## 4. Surprising Findings

### Against Product Assumptions:

1. **Packaging is a cancellation driver, NOT a primary purchase driver**
   - The Ditto study (January 2026) found: "Sustainability matters to me, but with meal kits the packaging is the sticking point" — but consumers TRIAL meal kits despite packaging concerns, then CANCEL because of post-promo pricing and subscription friction.
   - **Implication for Stàged:** Packaging as a primary value prop may attract already-frustrated ex-meal-kit users, but may not differentiate during initial consideration.

2. **Recipe app market is dominated by FREE users (73.6%)**
   - Market Growth Reports 2025: Free apps hold 73.6% share; paid app revenue potential limited.
   - **Implication:** Stàged's recipe/planning features may struggle to monetize directly — affiliate/grocery integration model may be more viable than premium subscription.

3. **Home cooking at peak pandemic levels — but for cost, not ideology**
   - Campbell's CEO (June 2025): "Consumers are cooking at home at the highest levels since early 2020" — driven by economic concerns, not sustainable living values.
   - **Implication:** Eco-positioning may be secondary to cost savings for most users. Stàged needs strong cost narrative alongside sustainability.

4. **Blue Apron abandoned subscriptions entirely (2025)**
   - Industry signal: Even the pioneer is moving away from recurring revenue model.
   - **Implication:** Subscription fatigue is real and worsening. Stàged should consider flexible/freemium model rather than strong subscription lock-in.

5. **HelloFresh in clear decline trajectory**
   - Q1 2025: -6.9% revenue YoY, double-digit margin focus over growth, shares under pressure.
   - **Implication:** Market is contracting for incumbents — window of opportunity for alternatives, but also signal that consumer appetite for meal kits themselves may be waning.

6. **US home cooking data shows education/income divide**
   - UNC study (2003-2023): Rise in home cooking driven by college graduates and higher-income; equity gaps widening.
   - **Implication:** Stàged's eco-conscious positioning may over-index on demos already cooking — need to consider value messaging for broader appeal.

## Summary & Recommendations

**Critical Adjustments Needed:**

1. **Retire the 14.5M tons claim** — Find a credible source or reframe. Consider: "Meal kits use 33% more packaging than equivalent grocery shopping" (per LCA studies).

2. **Reframe packaging value prop** — It's a cancellation driver, not a purchase driver. Lead with cost savings + convenience, sustainability as secondary differentiator.

3. **Watch the subscription model** — Even Blue Apron is moving away. Consider freemium + affiliate model rather than strong subscription commitment.

4. **Recipe app monetization will be hard** — 73.6% free users. Stàged's revenue model (affiliate commissions) may be more realistic than paid premium.

5. **Cost messaging is primary** — Home cooking is up because of economics, not ideology. Lead with "cook for $4-7/meal" not "save the planet."
