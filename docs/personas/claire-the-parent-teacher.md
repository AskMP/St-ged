## Frustrations

- Most "kid-friendly" recipe content is either craft-project food (decorating cookies) or so simple it's insulting to her 14-year-old

- Coordinating 5 schedules means weeknight cooking windows are narrow (45 min max)
- Most household meal planning tools don't have a "teaching mode" — they optimize for speed, which is the opposite of what she wants
