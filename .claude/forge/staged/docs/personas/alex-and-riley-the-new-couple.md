## Goals

- Establish a weekly cooking ritual — Wednesday night is "their night" in the kitchen together
- Find recipes that work as a two-person activity (parallel prep tasks, not one person stirring while the other watches)
- Explore cuisines they don't normally cook — it's an adventure as much as dinner
- Build a shared recipe repertoire they're both proud of
- Use cooking as connection — slowing down, putting phones away, making something together

## Frustrations

- Most recipes aren't designed for two active cooks — they assume one person is doing everything
- The "date night recipe" category online is usually either too easy (already know this) or too ambitious (needs professional equipment)
- They have two different cooking skill levels and two food memories — finding overlap takes negotiation
- Neither of them loves doing dishes, so recipes with fewer pots/pans win
- They both add things to grocery lists separately in different apps — coordination breaks down before the fun starts
