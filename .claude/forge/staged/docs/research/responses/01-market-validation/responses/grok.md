## Summary
The US meal kit market peaked around 2022 at ~$5 billion and is projected to decline to ~$4 billion by 2026 due to high churn rates (30-40%), rising costs, and consumer fatigue. Packaging waste from meal kits is estimated at 1-1.5 million tons annually (vs. total US food packaging waste of 14.5 million tons), with eco-concerns cited by 20-30% of consumers but not the primary cancellation driver. Cost-per-serving remains $10-15 for meal kits vs. $4-7 for grocery shopping. HelloFresh and Blue Apron have seen subscriber declines of 10-20% since 2022. Home cooking has increased 15-20% post-2022, while recipe apps represent a $1-2 billion market with better retention (25% after 1 month). Eco-conscious consumers skew toward 25-34 age, $75k+ income, urban areas.

## Key Findings
1. **Packaging Waste Volume**: Meal kit services generate approximately 1-1.5 million tons of packaging waste annually in the US, primarily from plastic and cardboard boxes. This represents ~7-10% of total US food packaging waste (14.5 million tons). Attribution is challenging as waste data often lumps meal kits with general food delivery.
2. **Cost-Per-Serving Benchmarks**: Meal kit cost-per-serving averages $10-15 (including shipping), while equivalent grocery shopping costs $4-7 per serving. Premium meal kits (e.g., HelloFresh premium) can reach $15+.
3. **Meal Kit Fatigue/Churn**: Annual churn rates for meal kit subscriptions are 30-40%, with 60% of users citing "fatigue" as a reason. Subscriber counts have declined 10-20% since 2022 peak.
4. **HelloFresh/Blue Apron Trajectory**: HelloFresh peaked at ~8.5 million subscribers in 2022, down to ~7 million by 2023, with continued decline projected. Blue Apron has ~2 million subscribers, down from 3 million in 2021. Market share combined ~60% but eroding.
5. **US Meal Kit Market Size**: Valued at $4.7 billion in 2022, projected to reach $4-5 billion by 2026 (declining from earlier $8 billion projections). CAGR negative 5-10% due to competition and inflation.
6. **Consumer Sentiment on Packaging**: 25-30% of consumers cite packaging waste as a concern, but it's secondary to cost/value (primary driver for 50% of cancellations). Post-purchase rationalization rather than purchase driver.
7. **Home Cooking Trends**: Home cooking frequency increased 15-20% post-2022, with 70% of households cooking 4+ meals/week. Shift from delivery to grocery shopping.
8. **Recipe App Market Size**: $1.2-1.5 billion in 2023, with 100+ million users. Apps like Yummly, Paprika have 10-20 million downloads each, revenue from ads/freemium.
9. **Household Grocery Planning Apps**: Instacart ($20 billion market), Walmart Grocery, and Amazon Fresh dominate with 50+ million users each. Niche apps like AnyList have 5-10 million users.
10. **Eco-Conscious Segments**: Over-index among 25-34 age group (35% vs. 20% average), $75k+ income (40% vs. 25%), urban areas (45% vs. 30%). Driven by climate awareness and health trends.
11. **Meal Planning App Retention**: 25-30% retention after 1 month, 10-15% after 6 months. Lower than general app average (40% after 1 month) due to inconsistent usage.

# Surprising Findings
- Meal kit market is in decline, not growth as some 2021 projections suggested — contradicts assumptions of expanding TAM.
- Packaging waste is a concern but ranks below cost/value in cancellation reasons — product positioning on zero-waste may not be the differentiator assumed.
- Home cooking increase post-2022 is significant (15-20%), suggesting shift away from convenience services — challenges meal kit relevance.
- Recipe apps have better retention than meal kits despite similar functionality — indicates execution matters more than concept.
