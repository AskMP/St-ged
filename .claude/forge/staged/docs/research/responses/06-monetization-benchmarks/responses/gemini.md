### 2. Break-Even Math (The Path to Sustainability)

*Based on a $113 Average Order Value (AOV) for Instacart and a 2% average fulfillment commission.*

| Target MRR | Required MAU (Affiliate Only) | Required MAU (Ad-Supported) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **$1,000** | ~450 Monthly Orders | ~65,000 Pageviews (@ $15 CPM) |
| **$10,000** | ~4,500 Monthly Orders | ~650,000 Pageviews |
| **$100,000** | ~45,000 Monthly Orders | ~6,500,000 Pageviews |

> **Inference:** At a 10% conversion rate (from recipe view to order), Stàged needs **450,000 Monthly Active Users** to generate **$100k/mo** purely through fulfillment. Adding CPG product placement (CPM) significantly lowers this threshold.

### 3. CPG Placement Landscape (2026 Trends)

CPG brands like **Kellanova (Special K)** and **Kerrygold** are shifting budgets from "Banner Ads" to **"Retail Media Layering."**

* **The "Kerrygold" Model:** Brands pay a premium (often $20+ CPM or a fixed monthly sponsorship) to be the "Default Ingredient" in specific recipe categories (e.g., "Butter" defaults to "Kerrygold Salted Butter" in the cart).
* **Shoppable Buttons:** Integration is the standard. 2026 best practices focus on **"Smart Bundling"**—adding an entire "Taco Night" kit (meat, shells, seasoning) to the cart in one tap, which increases AOV and commission.

---

### 4. Risk Flags

1. **The "1% Trap":** Amazon Fresh's 1% commission is a "loss leader" for them, but a "margin killer" for you. Relying solely on Amazon for fulfillment makes sustainability difficult without massive scale.
2. **Attribution Leakage:** If a user adds items to an Instacart cart via Stàged but closes the app and finishes the purchase 3 hours later on their desktop, the affiliate "cookie" may expire or be overwritten by a Google Search ad.
3. **Chef Unit Economics:** For **Stàge Events**, if a chef charges **$300/session**, you need 60 attendees at $5/ticket just to break even on talent. Corporate workshops (team-building) at $25–$50/head are a more viable high-margin path.
