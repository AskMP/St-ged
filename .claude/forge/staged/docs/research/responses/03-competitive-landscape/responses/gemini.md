### 2. Validated Gaps (Opportunities for Stàged)

* **Event & Potluck Coordination:** No major competitor has a dedicated "Event" mode that handles guest ingredient contributions or headcount-based recipe scaling for groups. AnyList and Samsung Food are strictly "household-inward."
* **Active "Zero-Waste" UI:** While **Mealime** and **Ollie** focus on ingredient overlap, none use an "Eco-Score" or active packaging-waste reduction filter as their primary brand identity.
* **Offline-First PWA:** Samsung Food and Ollie are heavily reliant on cloud/AI processing. A robust, offline-capable PWA for grocery aisles remains a technical differentiator for Stàged.

### 3. Closed Gaps (Corrected Assumptions)

* **AnyList Delivery Integration:** The brief assumes AnyList has "no delivery integration." This is **incorrect**. As of 2024–2026, AnyList supports **Instacart, Amazon Fresh, Walmart, and Kroger** fulfillment directly through their "Order Pickup or Delivery" feature.
* **Samsung Food Maturity:** Samsung Food (formerly Whisk) is no longer a simple recipe app. With **AI Vision (Gemini)** integration in 2026, it can read labels and recognize 50+ fridge items to suggest recipes, effectively competing with Stàged's AI-assisted planning.

### 4. Emerging Threats (2024–2026)

* **Ollie (Launched late 2024):** A direct threat. It markets itself as "Family-First AI" that handles budget, variety rules, and kid-friendly swaps. It is currently winning the "Mental Load" marketing battle.
* **FoodiePrep:** An AI-native planner that focuses on "Decision Support" (deciding what to cook based on pantry) rather than just organization.
* **PlateJoy (Exit):** **PlateJoy discontinued services on July 1, 2025**, leaving a significant market of premium, health-conscious meal planners looking for a new home.

### 5. User Voice: Mining the Dissatisfaction

Review data from Reddit (r/CookingProTips) and the App Store (2025–2026) highlight three recurring pain points that Stàged can exploit:

1. **"Subscription Bloat":** Users are revolting against $60+/year prices for Samsung Food+. Stàged's **affiliate-led (free for users)** model is a major competitive advantage.
2. **Sync Reliability:** Even in 2026, Samsung Food users complain about "Joined Lists" failing to sync in real-time at the store.
3. **The "5-Page Life Story":** Users still praise Paprika because it "scrapes" recipes cleanly. Any competitor that forces users to view ads or "blog filler" during the fulfillment process will lose.
