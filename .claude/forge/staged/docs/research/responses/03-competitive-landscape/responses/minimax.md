## 2. Validated Gaps (Confirmed Absent from All Competitors)

### ✅ Potluck/Party Coordination
**CONFIRMED:** No competitor offers dedicated potluck or event coordination features. Samsung Food has "Shared Meal Plans" (2021) but nothing for coordinating dishes across multiple guests for events.

### ✅ Eco/Zero-Waste Filtering
**CONFIRMED:** While Samsung Food published blog posts about "tackling food waste" (2024), there is NO dedicated eco-mode or sustainability filter that lets users search recipes by environmental impact, seasonal ingredients, or zero-waste criteria. This remains a genuine white space.

### ✅ Combined Household Sync + Recipe-to-Cart + Eco-Filtering
**CONFIRMED:** No app combines all three: shared household grocery lists, one-tap recipe-to-cart fulfillment, AND eco/zero-waste filtering. This is Stàged's primary defensible position if executed well.

## 3. Claims Requiring Refinement

### ⚠️ "Paprika - no delivery integration"
**STATUS: ACCURATE** — Paprika remains focused on recipe organization and manual list creation. No grocery delivery integration as of 2026.

### ⚠️ "AnyList - grocery-focused, no recipe-to-cart"
**STATUS: INACCURATE** — AnyList DOES have Instacart integration. Users can send lists to Instacart for delivery. However, it's primarily a grocery list app with recipe features as secondary.

### ⚠️ "HelloFresh/CookUnity - physical kit dependency"
**STATUS: ACCURATE** — Both require meal kit delivery. However, both now offer "market" or grocery add-ons, though this is secondary to their core model.

## 8. Strategic Recommendations

### For Stàged's Positioning:

1. **Primary Defensible Gap**: The combination of (a) household coordination + (b) recipe-to-cart + (c) eco-filtering is unique. Pursue this aggressively before competitors close the gap.

2. **Watch List**: Monitor **Zestyplan** (eco-positioning) and **Meals You Love** (recipe-to-cart + Kroger + Instacart) as direct competitors.

3. **Refine Claims**:
   - AnyList DOES have Instacart — adjust competitive messaging
   - Focus on potluck/event coordination as key differentiator (no competitor has this)
   - Emphasize eco-filtering as differentiator (Zestyplan is early but not established)

4. **Potential Partnership**: Consider Instacart API integration early (like Meals You Love) and Kroger partnership (like Foogal).
