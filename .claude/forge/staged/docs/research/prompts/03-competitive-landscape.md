# Research Prompt 03 — Competitive Landscape Deep Dive
**Platform:** Gemini Deep Research (primary) | Grok (App Store reviews, X/Reddit product discussions) | ChatGPT, Claude, BigPickle, Raptor Mini, MiniMax (cross-check)
**Topic:** Samsung Food/Whisk, Paprika, AnyList, and emerging competitors — actual feature gaps vs. claimed gaps

---

## Project Context

Stàged's competitive positioning claims these gaps:
- **Whisk/Samsung Food**: strong recipe-to-cart but lacks eco positioning and party/potluck coordination
- **Paprika/AnyList**: good UX but no delivery integration, often paid
- **HelloFresh/Cook Unity**: physical kit dependency, high cost, no flexibility

The product needs to validate that these gaps are real and current, and identify any emerging competitors that could close them before Stàged reaches scale.

---

## Claims to Verify

1. Samsung Food (formerly Whisk) — does it currently lack eco/zero-waste mode and potluck coordination?
2. Paprika App — no Instacart/delivery integration as of 2025–2026?
3. AnyList — grocery-focused, no recipe-to-cart fulfillment?
4. Are there any new entrants (2024–2026) that combine recipe management + household coordination + grocery delivery?
5. Is Meez truly B2B only, or has it launched a consumer product?

---

## Research Questions

1. What are Samsung Food's (Whisk's) current features as of 2025–2026, specifically: Instacart integration, household sharing, eco/sustainability filters, and event/potluck coordination?
2. Has Paprika App added any grocery delivery or fulfillment integration since 2023?
3. What is AnyList's current feature set — does it support recipe import, household sync, and any fulfillment?
4. Are there venture-backed recipe/meal planning apps that launched in 2024–2025 targeting the same space? (Check Product Hunt, TechCrunch, AngelList launches)
5. Has any major grocery retailer (Kroger, Walmart, Whole Foods) launched a competing meal planning + fulfillment experience natively?
6. What features do users most frequently request in reviews of Whisk, Paprika, AnyList, and Mealime? (App Store reviews, Reddit threads, Product Hunt comments)
7. Does any current consumer product combine: (a) shared household grocery lists, (b) recipe-to-cart fulfillment, and (c) eco/zero-waste filtering?
8. What is Mealime's current user base and feature set — does it compete directly?
9. Has Plan to Eat, Yummly, or BigOven added delivery integration or household coordination?
10. What happened to Whisk after Samsung acquisition — is it actively developed or in maintenance mode?
11. Are there any European or Asian market apps that already solve this combination that could expand to the US?
12. What are the most common reasons cited in public for meal planning app churn (Reddit, App Store reviews)?

---

## Output Format

1. **Feature comparison matrix** — Stàged's claimed gaps vs. each competitor's actual current state
2. **Validated gaps** — features genuinely absent from all competitors
3. **Closed gaps** — features Stàged claims are unique but competitors already have
4. **Emerging threats** — new entrants that could directly compete
5. **User voice** — what users say they want that nobody is building (from review mining)
6. **Source list** — App Store pages, Product Hunt, TechCrunch, Reddit threads, dated

---

## Research Standards

- Prefer primary sources first: official docs, earnings releases, API docs, legal text, platform policies, and first-party pricing pages.
- Separate sourced facts from inference. If you calculate or extrapolate a number, label it clearly as an inference.
- Include exact dates for time-sensitive claims and use current data where available.
- For every material claim, provide a URL or a formal citation.
- If a claim cannot be verified from credible public sources, say that explicitly instead of filling the gap with plausible language.
- If sources conflict, show the range and explain why the estimates may differ.
- Do not repeat assumptions from the brief unless they were independently verified in this research pass.
